Urban Fortifications

Ancient city walls in Nanjing meet London's historic towers — engineering feats that shaped city identity and defense strategies.

Engineering Marvels

Nanjing (Brick & Mortar): Utilized a massive rammed-earth core encased in kiln-fired bricks bonded with lime, rice flour, and tung oil—creating a flexible, seismic-resistant shell.
London (Stone Keep): Relied on thick, vertical limestone ashlar masonry to withstand siege engines and projectiles through sheer mass and gravity.
